## Structural Sell Rule Lock

- Structural sell formulas use base/segment/waist damage, MA-flow damage, volume-backed failure, and sell-pressure evidence.
- Fixed N% loss, fixed N% target, break-even recovery, and high-to-low N% trailing are not allowed in prepared sell formulas.
- Percent risk controls, if ever needed, must live outside strategy sell formulas as a separately labeled safety guard.
